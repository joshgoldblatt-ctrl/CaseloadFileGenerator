Calculation Methodology

Student Type Grouping

Students are grouped into two main categories for breakout analysis:

New — Includes students with a Student Type of "New" or "Transfer"

Continuing — Includes students with a Student Type of "Continuing" or "Readmit"

Not Found — Shown as its own category in the overview but not included in the New or Continuing groups

All students, regardless of Student Type, are included in overall/total calculations.

Multi-Office & Multi-Advisor Students

Some students are assigned to more than one advising office or more than one advisor. In the per-office view, these students are counted in every office they are assigned to. For example, a student assigned to both ASAP and College Discovery will appear as +1 student in each office's row.

Similarly, in the per-advisor view, if a student is assigned to two advisors, they are counted toward both advisors' caseloads.

When two advisors are assigned from the same office (e.g., two ISS advisors), the student is only counted once for that office, not twice.

Assigned Advisor Rate (Per Office)

For each office: of all students assigned to that office, what percentage met with at least one of their assigned advisors who belongs to that office. The check uses the officeToAdvisors mapping — it verifies that the advisor the student met is both (a) assigned to the student and (b) associated with that specific office. Each student is counted only once per office regardless of how many appointments they had.

Office Appointment Rate (Per Office)

For each office: of all students assigned to that office, what percentage had at least one appointment at that office location. This checks the appointment's Location field, not the advisor. So if a student met their ISS advisor at a different location (e.g., Student Advising Services), it would count for the assigned advisor rate but not the office appointment rate.

Any Advisor from Office Rate

For each office: of all students assigned to that office, what percentage met with any advisor from that office — not necessarily their specific assigned advisor. This captures cases where a student saw a colleague of their assigned advisor within the same office.

Advisor-Level Metrics (By Advisor View & Export)

Total Students — Number of students assigned to this advisor on the caseload

Assigned Appointments (unduplicated) — Number of unique assigned students who had at least one advising appointment with this advisor (each student counted once, even if they visited multiple times)

Assigned Rate — Assigned Appointments ÷ Total Students × 100

Total Appointments (duplicated) — Raw count of all advising appointments conducted by this advisor, counting every visit including repeat visits from the same student

Total Appts w/ Assigned — Of the total appointments, how many were with students who are assigned to this advisor (duplicated — counts every visit)

% Appts w/ Assigned Students — Total Appts w/ Assigned ÷ Total Appointments × 100

Appointment Frequency Distribution

For each advisor or office, shows the distribution of how many advising appointments each assigned student had: 0 appointments, 1 appointment, 2 appointments, 3 appointments, or 4+ appointments. Percentages are calculated against the total number of assigned students for that advisor or office.

Service Filtering

The app distinguishes between true advising services and other services (e.g., administrative tasks, workshops). Only appointments matching the configured advising services are counted toward advising metrics. Non-advising services are excluded from all rate calculations. The service filter can be configured in the Advising Services Configuration section.

Grand Total Rows

Important: Grand total rows are calculated differently than per-office or per-advisor rows.

Office view — "GRAND TOTAL (Unduplicated)": The grand total uses a deduplicated count of all students. A student assigned to two offices (e.g., ASAP and College Discovery) is counted only once in the grand total, even though they appear in both office rows above. The assigned advisor rate in the grand total asks: of all unique students, what % met any of their assigned advisors anywhere? The office appointment rate asks: of all unique students, what % had an appointment at any of their assigned offices? Because of this deduplication, the grand total will not equal the sum of the per-office rows — this is expected behavior.

Advisor view: There is no grand total row in the advisor view or export, because students can be assigned to multiple advisors, making a simple sum misleading.

Frequency tables — "TOTAL (Unduplicated)": The total row deduplicates students across all advisors or offices. A student shared between two advisors is counted once in the total, categorized by their total number of appointments with any advisor in that group.

Student Type / Credit / GPA totals: These TOTAL rows are additive sums of the per-category counts above them. Since students belong to only one Student Type, credit range, or GPA range, there is no duplication concern and the total equals the sum of the rows.

Comparison / Difference Columns

When a previous semester's data is loaded, "Diff" columns show the change from the prior semester to the current semester. For rate columns, this is the percentage point difference (current rate minus prior rate). For count columns (e.g., Total Appointments), this is the raw count difference. A positive number means an increase from the prior semester.

Weekly Appointment Data (By Date)

The By Date tab groups all appointments by week, with weeks starting on Monday and labeled by the Monday date (e.g., "Week of 2/3/25"). This uses all filtered appointments — if you apply filters (office, service, student type, etc.), the weekly counts will recalculate to reflect only matching appointments. The office breakout toggle shows a stacked bar chart and per-office columns in the tables. When comparison data is loaded, prior semester weeks are shown side by side. The weekly export includes both tables with office-level breakout columns.

First Appointments by Week: Counts unique students having their first appointment each week. Each student is counted only once, in the week of their earliest appointment. If a student's first appointment is on 9/3, they count toward the "Week of 9/1" row and do not appear in any subsequent week. The chart visualizes this data.

Total Appointment Volume by Week: Counts all appointments each week, including repeat visits from the same student. This is a raw volume count.

Data Filters

Filters allow you to narrow the analysis to specific subsets of students or appointments. All filters are multi-select — you can pick any combination of values. When applied, the entire analysis recalculates using only the matching data. The Student Type filter operates on the caseload data and filters students by their individual Student Type value (New, Transfer, Continuing, Readmit, Not Found). This is different from the Student Type grouping used in breakout charts, which combines types into groups.
